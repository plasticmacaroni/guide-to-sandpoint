# Season of Ghosts — The Funny Stuff

A catalog of the funny, charming, absurd, and darkly-comic moments across all 43 sessions,
mined straight from the table's own notes. Tags: [quote] [event] [name] [gag] [dark]
[wholesome] [meta].

## Running gags (the throughlines)
- **Aeson one-shots everything** — wolves, toads, rokurokubi, scorpions… in a single swing.
- **Yami the cat leaves weird porch gifts** — a potato, glasses, two platinum, a silver dagger…
- **Tea & snacks solve every social encounter** — even the Nosoi love J'nan's tea.
- **The stolen pitcher → "The Broken Pitchers"** — Orlen's pilfered pitcher becomes his band's name. [NOTE: this is the most important one]
- **Shinzo's tablecloth** — Death's grand mystical gift to Aeson is… a lovely tablecloth.
